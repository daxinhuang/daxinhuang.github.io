Daxin HUANG

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Mathematics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) under the supervision of Prof. Shuoqing Deng. I earned my MSc in Financial Mathematics at HKUST and my BSc in Financial Mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

Research

My research focuses on stochastic control, optimal stopping, and their applications in mathematical finance. I am also interested in robust finance, particularly model-independent pricing and hedging problems.
